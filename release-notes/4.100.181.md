## The lead answers you while its agents run, and reads every agent's report

- **A message sent during a round is answered right away.** The lead read
  your messages only between its own turns. During `spawn_agent` it sits
  inside a single tool call until every agent finishes, so a message sent
  45 minutes into a round of 75 was queued and never read. Now a message
  that arrives while agents are running gets a short extra turn at once:
  the lead's own model, its conversation, the round's live state and your
  message. The lead can reply, pass a message to some or all running
  agents (`message_agents`, read at their next turn), or stop some or all
  of them (`stop_agents`). The reply appears in the chat. Once the round
  returns, the lead's next turn is told what was said and done. If the
  side turn fails, your message is queued as before.
- **Every agent's report reaches the lead.** A round's reports went back as
  one tool result, and the lead is sent at most 32,000 characters of any
  tool result, cut from the middle. On a round of 75 it saw 26 reports.
  Now, once an agent is done, it is asked for a summary of at most 1,000
  characters. The lead gets every summary, and reads any full report with
  the new `read_agent_report(name)`, up to 200 lines per call, paging
  through a long report like a large file. A report already under 1,000
  characters is its own summary and costs no extra turn.
