## Delegated agents use their own thinking setting, and refreshed templates

- **An Agents tab's or node's thinking setting now reaches its agents.** The
  connection built for the Agents tab and for each agent node carried the
  provider, model, key and PolyKV settings, but not the tab's reasoning,
  sampler or output cap. So every delegated agent ran with the **lead's**.
  Measured on a swarm run: the Agents tab (opencoti) had thinking off, the lead
  (ollama) had it on at high, and the opencoti agents ran with a 32,000-token
  thinking budget against the lead's 64,000-token cap. One agent thought for 11
  minutes, and a wave of 30 took 20 minutes behind its slowest members. Now a
  tab that states thinking, temperature or an output budget is what its agents
  use. A tab that states none of them still follows the session.
- **An Agents tab's sampler reaches the engine.** The tab's temperature and
  penalties were not in the agents' connection either, so an opencoti node
  sampled with the server's defaults whatever the tab said.
- **Four prompt templates refreshed.** Sections that had fallen behind the
  built-in tool descriptions were rewritten:
  - deepseek: `read_files`, `editor`, `ask_question`, `check_file`,
    `spawn_agent`
  - kimi-k3: `read_files`, `editor`, `check_file`
  - minimax: `ask_question`, `spawn_agent`
  - qwen: `check_file`

  Among other things they now describe the `agents` batch form of
  `spawn_agent`, the recommended-option rule of `ask_question`, and what the
  delimiter scan reports.
- **Compaction prompts in templates:** a near-copy of a compaction prompt (a
  one-word change, or the same sentences reordered) is now left out of a
  generated template, like an exact copy, so it keeps using the built-in.
