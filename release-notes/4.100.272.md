## Sound effects

- **`generate_sound_effect` makes a sound from a description and saves it in
  the workspace:** a button click, a notification, a game event. It is set up
  under **Offer sound effects** on the Audio tab, needs a model made for this,
  and works in the plugin and the CLI (`soundEffects` in a `--media-config`
  file). Leave its endpoint empty to use the text-to-speech server. It needs
  xOllama v0.40.13-xollama.1 or later, or opencoti; older servers refuse it.
