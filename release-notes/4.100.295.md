## Find a skill from the chat box

- **`/skill` followed by words finds a skill.** Start a message with `/skill`
  and type words from the skill's name or description: the menu above the
  chat box lists the enabled skills that have every word, in any order and
  any case, with their descriptions. The arrows move through it, and Enter
  or Tab (or a click) replaces `/skill …` with the skill's command, for
  example `/docker-compose-deploy `, ready for the request. With nothing
  matching it says so, and Enter does nothing rather than send a `/skill`
  no skill answers to. Escape closes it. It works the way `/delegate`
  offers agents.
- **Skills are in the `/` menu.** Typing `/` lists the enabled skills under
  **Skills**, after the other commands, and a skill matches anywhere in its
  name: `/deploy` finds `docker-compose-deploy`. A skill typed in full is
  highlighted as a command, like a workflow.
- A skill switched off on the Skills tab is not offered by either. What
  happens on sending is unchanged: the message keeps the typed
  `/<skill-name>`, and the model loads that skill with its skills tool.
